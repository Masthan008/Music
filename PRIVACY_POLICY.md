# Privacy Policy for Sound Center

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

**Sound Center** ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Masthan as an open-source, user-friendly music and audio player application. This Privacy Policy informs you regarding our policies concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use Sound Center.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

Sound Center is built with privacy in mind. **We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).**

#### A. Device Storage and Media Access
- **Permission:** `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`
- **Purpose:** Sound Center requests access to your device's audio files strictly to index, organize, and play your local music files, audiobooks, and songs. 
- **Processing:** All scanning and playback occurs entirely offline on your local device. None of your files, playlists, or audio metadata are ever transmitted to or stored on external servers.

#### B. Internet Access
- **Permission:** `INTERNET`
- **Purpose:** Used solely for online playback features initiated by the user:
  - Streaming online radio stations.
  - Fetching podcast RSS feeds and streaming podcast episodes.
  - Searching and streaming cloud tracks from SoundCloud.
  - Downloading remote album artwork for streams.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services

When you stream online content, your device connects directly to the respective service providers:
- **SoundCloud API / Public Streams:** Subject to SoundCloud's Terms of Use.
- **Public Radio Directory / Streams:** Direct connections to public broadcast URLs.
- **Podcast RSS Hosts:** Connections to public RSS feed servers.

Sound Center does not inject advertising trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage

All your application preferences, custom playlists, favorite radio stations, and theme configurations are stored locally on your device via secure local storage. You can delete all stored data at any time by:
1. Clearing app data via your device's Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > Sound Center > Storage > Clear Data`).
2. Uninstalling the application.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy

Sound Center does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general-purpose audio player suitable for all ages.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
